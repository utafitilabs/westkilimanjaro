The outlook and the ocean signals are the same across West Kilimanjaro: the seasonal models work on grid cells about 110 km wide, and the nine wards rise and fall together from year to year. What differs between wards is how much rain they normally get and how much has fallen recently; those figures are in the next section.Mwelekeo wa msimu na ishara za bahari ni sawa kwa West Kilimanjaro yote: modeli za msimu zinatumia visanduku vya gridi vya upana wa takriban km 110, na kata tisa zinapanda na kushuka pamoja mwaka hadi mwaka. What 'measured' means hereMaana ya 'kilichopimwa' hapa

  • Rain: CHIRPS, satellite estimates calibrated with rain gauges, averaged over the ward (5.5 km cells).Mvua: CHIRPS, makadirio ya setilaiti yaliyorekebishwa kwa vipimo vya mvua, wastani wa kata (visanduku vya km 5.5).
  • Solar radiation: derived from Meteosat satellite images (EUMETSAT), at the village point.Mionzi ya jua: kutoka picha za setilaiti ya Meteosat (EUMETSAT), kwenye kijiji.
  • Temperature and humidity: ERA5, ECMWF's reanalysis, which blends observations into a model on a 30 km grid. No weather station reports from inside the nine wards, so this is the best available reference, not a direct measurement.Joto na unyevu: ERA5, uchambuzi upya wa ECMWF unaochanganya vipimo na modeli kwenye gridi ya km 30. Hakuna kituo cha hali ya hewa ndani ya kata tisa, hivyo hiki ndicho kipimo bora kilichopo, si kipimo cha moja kwa moja.
  • Forecasts: the forecasts as actually issued one and three days before each date (Open-Meteo's archive of past model runs), not re-run afterwards.Utabiri: utabiri kama ulivyotolewa siku moja na tatu kabla ya kila tarehe (kumbukumbu za Open-Meteo za modeli zilizopita), si uliorudiwa baadaye.
  • Albedo (the share of sunlight the ground reflects) is planned: the MODIS albedo product needs NASA Earthdata access.Albedo (sehemu ya mwanga wa jua unaoakisiwa na ardhi) inapangwa: bidhaa ya albedo ya MODIS inahitaji ruhusa ya NASA Earthdata.

Kata zinapanda na kushuka pamoja mwaka hadi mwaka (uhusiano 0.89–1.00), hivyo hii ni ishara moja kwa eneo lote, si ya kila kata peke yake.

5El Niño and the Indian OceanEl Niño na Bahari ya Hindi

East Africa's short rains respond to two oceans. A warm central Pacific (El Niño) and a warm western Indian Ocean relative to the east (a positive Indian Ocean Dipole) both move rain-bearing air towards the region; this year both are in place.Mvua za vuli Afrika Mashariki zinategemea bahari mbili. 6For farmers and herdersKwa wakulima na wafugaji

What the short rains mean on the ground. General guidance only; local advice comes first.Maana ya mvua za vuli shambani na malishoni. Ni mwongozo wa jumla; ushauri wa wataalamu wa eneo lako unatangulia.

PlantingKupanda

Plant once the rains have set in and the soil is moist; the first showers of October often stop again for a week or more. Ask your ward extension officer which varieties suit the season.Panda baada ya mvua kuanza kwa uhakika na udongo kuwa na unyevu; mara nyingi mvua za kwanza za Oktoba hukatika kwa wiki moja au zaidi. Muulize afisa ugani wa kata yako aina za mbegu zinazofaa msimu huu.

Soil and slopesUdongo na miteremko

Heavy rain on the steep slopes washes away topsoil. Repair contour ridges and grass strips, and clear drainage channels before the peak of the season in November.Mvua kubwa kwenye miteremko mikali huondoa udongo wa juu. Tengeneza matuta ya kontua na vipande vya nyasi, na safisha mifereji kabla ya kilele cha msimu mwezi Novemba.

Livestock and pastureMifugo na malisho

Pasture on the plains towards Tingatinga should recover quickly. Heavy El Niño rains in East Africa have been followed by Rift Valley fever outbreaks (1997–98, 2006–07); follow the district veterinary officer's advice on vaccination.Malisho katika tambarare kuelekea Tingatinga yanatarajiwa kustawi haraka. Mvua kubwa za El Niño Afrika Mashariki ziliwahi kufuatiwa na milipuko ya homa ya Bonde la Ufa (1997–98, 2006–07); fuata ushauri wa afisa mifugo wa wilaya kuhusu chanjo.

Rivers and roadsMito na barabara

Seasonal streams coming off the mountain can rise within an hour of a storm upslope, even when it is dry where you are. Do not cross flowing water on foot or by vehicle.Mito ya msimu inayoshuka kutoka mlimani inaweza kujaa ndani ya saa moja baada ya mvua kubwa juu mlimani, hata kama hakuna mvua mahali ulipo. Usivuke maji yanayotiririka kwa miguu wala kwa gari.
